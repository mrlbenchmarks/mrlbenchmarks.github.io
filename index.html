The MRL Shared Task series creates community-built multilingual benchmarks that push the frontier of evaluation for large multilingual models. Our 2025 edition produced Global PIQA, a physical commonsense reasoning benchmark spanning over 100 languages across five continents.

The 2026 Shared Task is the next chapter. We are inviting researchers and language community members to shape the task focus. All accepted submission authors will be included on the resulting benchmark paper. We especially encourage participation from speakers of low-resource languages and non-prestige varieties.

2025 Results Now Available

Global PIQA v0.1 was released in October 2025. Explore the dataset on Hugging Face or read the preprint. See the 2025 Shared Task page for full details.

Task Format

Detailed task specifications for 2026 will be announced in early 2026. Based on the success of Global PIQA, the 2026 task will continue to prioritize community-created, culturally-grounded evaluation data for non-English languages. Stay tuned and register your interest below.

What to Expect

Dataset Submissions

Contribute evaluation datasets for your language(s). Detailed format requirements TBA.

Paper Track

Submit a dataset description paper alongside your data. Short papers welcome.

Benchmark Paper

All accepted contributors join as co-authors on the resulting compiled benchmark paper.

2025 Data Format (Reference)

The 2025 task used PIQA-style format. 2026 format details will be released soon.
